# Example project: sensor comparison

**Fictional teaching example.**

## Question and status

What explains a changing difference between a temperature sensor and a reference thermometer? This example project is exploratory; it has no established causal conclusion.

## Evidence and current interpretation

The [changing-temperature pilot](/sources/example-temperature-observation.md) and [fixed-temperature follow-up](/sources/example-fixed-temperature-observation.md) suggest checking environmental and warm-up effects before attributing the change to [drift](/concepts/example-concept.md).

The [analysis](/analyses/example-analysis.md) separates these explanations and proposes the next comparison. That comparison is a plan, not a completed experiment.

## Contribution boundaries

The observations and study design are invented for this template. They are not a publication, a validated method, or a claim of novelty. A real project page should identify who produced the evidence and who contributed each idea.

The [synthesis](/syntheses/example-synthesis.md) records the limited conclusion supported by the two example observations.
