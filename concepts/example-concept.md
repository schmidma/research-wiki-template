# Example concept: sensor drift

**Concept page for a fictional teaching example, not a literature-backed definition.**

For the [example project](/entities/example-project.md), I use *drift* as a working term for a change in a sensor's measurement characteristics over time under otherwise comparable conditions.

A changing difference between two instruments is not sufficient to establish drift in either instrument. Environmental conditions, warm-up, and the reference itself may also change. This distinction is central to the [analysis](/analyses/example-analysis.md).
